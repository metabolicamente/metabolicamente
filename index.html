What is metabolic psychiatry?

An approach that understands mental health as a result of brain metabolism, integrating the biology of energy, nutrition, and daily rhythms into the treatment of psychiatric conditions.

Conventional approach
Lifestyle is an afterthought
Our approach
Metabolic approach
Biology is the foundation

The brain is a metabolic organ.

It weighs about 2% of your body, yet consumes roughly 20% of your energy. When that energy system falters, your mind feels it first.

Energy production

Your brain depends on efficient energy production in every one of its cells, every second.

Nutrient supply

Neurotransmitters are built from what you eat. Nutrition is not a detail. It is raw material.

Metabolic balance

Insulin resistance, inflammation, and mitochondrial dysfunction can directly impact mood, focus, and stability.
